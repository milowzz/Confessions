Category

A moderator reads every post before it appears. Naming someone, contact details or anything that identifies a person gets rejected.

Guidelines

Post anything you want about your own life. Don't post about someone else's.

Always rejected

  • Real names, usernames, or enough detail to identify one person
  • Phone numbers, emails, addresses, class sections, schedules
  • Threats, harassment, or anything encouraging harm to a person
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18
  • Accusations of a crime against an identifiable person
  • Spam, advertising, or the same post over and over

How it works

Every post is read by a moderator before it appears. Nothing publishes automatically. Expect a wait.

Rejected posts are not published and not shown to anyone else. There is no appeal and no notification — rewrite it and post again.

What anonymous means here

No account, no email, no name. Nothing shown on a post identifies who wrote it, and other readers cannot find out.

The site does keep an anonymous browser ID with each submission, readable only by the moderator, so a repeat abuser can be blocked. It is not linked to your name and it is not published.

Something about you is up here

Use the report link on the post, or email the site owner. Say which post. It comes down while it is reviewed.

If you are struggling
